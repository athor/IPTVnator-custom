# IPTVnator Custom

## What's different from the official version?

- **Switch players above the video.** Choose Video.js, HTML5, ArtPlayer or Embedded MPV without opening Settings or leaving the current channel and category. Your choice is saved. Embedded MPV appears only on supported runtimes.
- **See the actual playback resolution.** The displayed dimensions come from the decoder, rather than the channel's name. Windows native-view MPV also reports its decoded resolution.
- **Open stream information.** A button above the video shows the available resolution, codecs, frame rate, bitrate, audio channels, container and buffer information, depending on what the player reports.
- **Keep original M3U category names and order.** Category names retain their capitalization and follow the playlist's order.
- **Read full category names.** Long labels wrap, and channel counts appear in rounded badges.
- **Wider category and channel columns.** Resizable M3U group panes provide more room for names and logos. Channel logos remain visible in the grouped list.

The changes do not upscale streams or add a manual quality selector to Embedded MPV.
